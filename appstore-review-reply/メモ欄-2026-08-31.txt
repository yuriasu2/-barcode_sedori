HOW TO TEST THIS APP
No account or login is required: no registration, sign-in or deletion flow, and no user-generated content.

Allow camera access and point it at a barcode (JAN / EAN-13), or use the search field with any code above (it also takes a 10-digit ISBN-10). The result shows product info, offer prices, sales rank and price graphs; scroll for the profit calculation.

WHY WE CANNOT PROVIDE A DEMO ACCOUNT
Three features need the user's own Seller Central account, linked via Amazon's OAuth: the per-seller offer list, listing-restriction warnings, and listing creation. Amazon's Data Protection Policy forbids sharing Selling Partner credentials, and these features can create real listings, so a shared test account is not viable; a screen recording of all three with our own seller account is provided instead. Unlinked, they show a lock icon and "Amazon account linking required"; the app does not crash.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A sourcing-research tool for Amazon Japan sellers, who have seconds in a store to judge whether an item is worth reselling. Japanese UI only, Amazon.co.jp only, Japan-only distribution.
