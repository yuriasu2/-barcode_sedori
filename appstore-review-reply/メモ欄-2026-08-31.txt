WHY WE CANNOT PROVIDE A DEMO ACCOUNT
Three features need the user's own Seller Central account, linked via Amazon's OAuth: the per-seller offer list, listing-restriction warnings, and listing creation. Amazon's Data Protection Policy forbids sharing Selling Partner credentials, and these can create real listings, so a shared test account is not viable; a screen recording is provided instead. Unlinked, they show a lock icon and a message asking to link; the app does not crash.
