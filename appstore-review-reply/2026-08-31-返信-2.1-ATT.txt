WHAT WENT WRONG WAS OUR REVIEW NOTES, NOT THE IMPLEMENTATION

App Tracking Transparency is implemented and works, including on iPadOS. The reason it could not be located is a mistake in the notes we gave you.

The request appears after four successful searches. Our notes said so, but listed only three sample product codes. Following them exactly stops at three searches, one short of the threshold, so the request never appears. On an iPad, where scanning real barcodes is impractical, those codes were the only practical way to search, so the shortfall was decisive. This was our error.

2. Perform four successful searches. Either scan four different product barcodes, or - with no products at hand - type these codes into the search field one at a time, searching after each:

3. On the fourth successful search a short explanation appears with a button to continue. Tapping it shows Apple's ATT dialog.

WHY THE REQUEST IS NOT ON FIRST LAUNCH

We show it after the user has used the app, so the request is made in context rather than on a cold first launch. No tracking data is collected before it: while the status is undetermined the IDFA is unavailable to us and ads are served non-personalised. Our App Store privacy declaration reflects that we do track once permission is granted, so we have not changed it.

We have also added, for a future update, a permanent entry in the app's Settings that opens the ATT dialog directly, so the request can always be reached without performing four searches. It is not in the build now under review.
